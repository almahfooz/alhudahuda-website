Request Account Deletion

alhudahuda – Your Online Arabian Marketplace

If you have an alhudahuda account in Malaysia or Nigeria, you can request deletion of your account and the personal information associated with it. You can submit your request even if you have uninstalled the app.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the alhudahuda app and sign in.
  2. Open the Delete Account option from your account/profile settings.
  3. Read the deletion notice and complete the verification and confirmation steps shown in the app.
  4. Submit your request. The app will display the result returned by the service.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

  1. Send an email to support@alhudahuda.com with the subject alhudahuda Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the registered phone number or email address and the country of your account (Malaysia or Nigeria).
  3. Our support team may ask you to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password, OTP, card details, or other secrets.
  4. After verification, the team will process the deletion request and communicate the outcome to you.
Email an account deletion request

What information is deleted?

When your request is approved and processed, we delete or de-identify account information that is no longer needed, which can include your profile, saved addresses, authentication records, device notification information, and support information associated with the deleted account, subject to necessary legal and operational exceptions.

What information may be kept?

Some order, payment, wallet, merchant or delivery records and limited related information may need to be retained for transaction accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention, security or applicable legal obligations. Where retention is legally or operationally necessary, the retention period depends on the relevant record and applicable law. Once there is no valid reason to retain it, the information will be deleted or de-identified as appropriate.

Deleting your alhudahuda account is not the same as deactivating it. You may lose access to orders, saved details and marketplace services connected with the account.

Questions or individual data requests

To ask about access to or correction or deletion of particular personal information without closing your account, email support@alhudahuda.com. Whether a particular request can be fulfilled depends on applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements.
