    Explore the onboarding guide
    What is alhudahuda?

    alhudahuda is an online Arabian marketplace that brings customers, merchants and delivery partners together through a mobile app. It helps people discover Arabian shops, products and everyday favourites in their local market.

    Can I shop on this website?

    Shopping takes place in the alhudahuda Android and iOS app. This website introduces our community, explains how to get started and helps you prepare for onboarding.

    Where is alhudahuda available?

    Our current markets are Malaysia and Nigeria. Service coverage, ordering and onboarding availability depend on your city and the current rollout. Oman and Indonesia are coming soon; launch dates have not yet been announced.

    How do I download the app?

    Use the Android and iOS options in the app section. Official store links will appear there when available. If you are taking part in onboarding, follow the installation instructions provided by the alhudahuda team.

    How do merchants and delivery partners join?

    Choose your role in the Join us section and open the onboarding checklist. Prepare the relevant business or delivery details, then follow the registration and verification steps shared by the alhudahuda team for your market.

    Which payments and delivery options are supported?

    Payment methods, checkout availability, delivery coverage and any charges depend on your market. Check the options displayed in the app and confirm them during onboarding. Some services may still be in a pilot rollout.

    Where can I get help during onboarding?

    Ask your alhudahuda onboarding representative for the official support channel for your market. The team can guide you through app access, registration and any verification steps.

    About this website

    Website privacy

    This website introduces alhudahuda and provides onboarding information. It does not collect registration details, accept orders or process payments.

    Your language preference is stored in your browser so that the website remembers English or Arabic on your next visit. Checklist selections are temporary and are not sent to our team.

    This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The hosting service may process basic connection information to serve and protect the website.

    The mobile app is a separate service. Review the policies and terms presented in the app before registering or using its services.
