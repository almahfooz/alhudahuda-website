Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how alhudahuda (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles personal information when people use the alhudahuda Android or iOS application and related marketplace services in Malaysia and Nigeria. The marketing website has separate, limited functionality.

1. Information we collect

Depending on your role and the features you use, we may process:

  • Account and profile details: name, email address, phone number, country, profile photograph, authentication and verification records.
  • Shopping and delivery details: saved addresses, selected products, orders, transaction status, delivery instructions and communication relating to an order.
  • Merchant and delivery-partner details: applications, merchant business and payment-profile information, product listings, delivery assignments, and information submitted for eligibility or verification.
  • Location data: location chosen or permitted when you use maps, delivery-address selection, route or delivery functions. Delivery partners may submit location updates during active delivery functions where supported.
  • Payment and wallet records: amounts, order and wallet references, payment-method selections, payment status and related transaction history. Available methods differ by market and rollout stage.
  • Support and communications: messages to customer support, order chat, notifications and related records.
  • Device and operational data: notification tokens and technical information used for app operation, security and troubleshooting.

We do not ask you to provide your payment-card details directly to the alhudahuda support team. Where an external payment provider is offered, its payment page and privacy practices apply to the information you give that provider.

2. How we use information

We use information to register and authenticate accounts; show marketplace products; operate carts and orders; coordinate merchants and delivery partners; process and verify supported payments or wallet entries; provide maps, customer support, and account or order notifications; protect the platform; resolve disputes; and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Location and device permissions

Some delivery and map features may request access to device location. The app uses location where needed for the feature you choose, such as identifying an address, estimating delivery distance, or supporting an active delivery. You can control location and notification permissions through device settings. Availability and the precise permissions requested depend on your role, operating system, and application version.

4. When information is shared

We may disclose information relevant to fulfilling a transaction to the merchant, customer, or assigned delivery partner involved. We also use service providers for hosting and databases, app notifications, maps, communications, and payment processing. Providers may process information on our behalf or under their own privacy terms. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users, investigate fraud or enforce lawful rights.

Where offered in Nigeria, Paystack may process payment-related information and return transaction status and reference data to us. Test-mode transactions do not constitute a live cash-deposit or withdrawal service.

5. Storage, security and international processing

We use access controls, authentication, and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No electronic service is completely secure. Our services and providers may process or store information outside your country, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

We retain information as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts and services, maintain transactions, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention needs may vary across different records and jurisdictions.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You may update certain account information, manage saved delivery addresses, and change notification or location permissions. To request account deletion, use the available account-deletion option inside the alhudahuda app, or email support@alhudahuda.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. We will delete or de-identify personal information where appropriate, except for information we must retain for legitimate transaction, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal reasons.

You can also contact us to request access to or correction of your personal data, subject to applicable law.

8. Children

alhudahuda is a general marketplace and is not designed specifically for children. The service is intended for users who meet the applicable requirements to hold an account and transact in their marketplace.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The effective date above indicates the current version of this page. Significant changes may also be communicated through the app where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or account deletion, contact support@alhudahuda.com. Website: alhudahuda.com.
